## Findings, most severe first

FINDING 1: [B] [MEDIUM] Cost proxy has a real, non-trivial reversal rate that the weak-lower-bound framing glosses over. Dynamically tested against real L2, confirmed and quantified.
Evidence: pulled real order-book best_bid from the pmxt L2 archive at _DataMetricPulls/pmxt_pulled, confirmed present from 2026-04-13, for all 22 Elon 2-day sealed-span auctions across all ladder buckets with L2 coverage (n=179 price-points at the T=start+24h checkpoint), and diffed against the canonical prices.close proxy the script actually uses.
Results: mean(proxy minus real_bid) = +0.0022, i.e. proxy overstates true buy cost on average, the direction the script claims. Median is about 0.000, with 25.1% of pairs exactly equal (thin, no-trade brackets where close IS the resting quote). 61.5% of pairs show proxy greater than bid, the claimed direction. 13.4% of pairs show proxy LESS than bid, the OPPOSITE of the claimed weak-lower-bound direction. Std is 0.0108, tail range -0.048 to +0.068.
Worst reversal: elon-musk-of-tweets-june-6-june-8, bracket 90-114: proxy close 0.012 vs real best_bid 0.060, a 5x understatement of true buy cost, exactly the opposite of conservative-in-our-favor.
Why it matters: the script docstring (lines 28-40) and RUN_META fills field assert that true achievable cost is very likely at or below this proxy, and that any positive EV here should be read as a weak lower bound. That holds on average and on the median, but is false for about 1 in 7 sampled price-points, with reversals up to 5x on thin, high-rung brackets. More importantly, the mean bias (about 0.2 cents) and its std (about 1.1 cents) are the same order of magnitude as the tiny efficiency-null point estimates the study reports (Elon pooled gap -0.20 to -0.27 cents per dollar, Trump -0.66 to -2.48 cents per dollar). This means the specific sign and size of the pooled market-overpays-or-underpays gap should be read as sitting inside the noise floor of the fill-realism assumption, not as a precise, tradeable mispricing estimate.
Why it is not fatal: it is disclosed prominently in both the script docstring and RUN_META fills, not hidden. It does not touch either headline hit-rate, 34.3% or 82.1%, both pure win-loss counts with no price involved. It does not touch the one CI-excluding-zero result in the whole study, Trump 80-99 going 0-for-13 sealed, which is also a pure hit-rate statistic. The qualitative conclusion that no combo beats cost and the market is close to efficient (slope 0.97 to 1.00, R2 0.76 to 0.97) is an order of magnitude larger effect than this proxy noise and survives. The larger single-bracket mispricing numbers, Trump 140-159 at -9.4 cents and Elon 65-89 and 140-164 at -3.5 cents, are 3 to 10 times the measured proxy bias and more likely to survive a hardened-cost re-score than the pooled gap.
Fix for any future fills-verified follow-up, not required to clear this audit: re-score the SEALED span, about 92% L2-covered from 2026-04-13 onward, against actual resting-bid depth instead of last-print close before sizing any real order off this study.
